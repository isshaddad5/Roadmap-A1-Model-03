Part 2: Reading (5 Marks)
My Weekly Routine

My name is Sarah. I am 25 years old and I am from Canada, but I live in London now. I am an office worker. From Monday to Friday, I get up at 7 o’clock. I usually have breakfast at 7:30. I go to work by bus because I don’t have a car. At work, I can use a computer and I can speak English and French. In the evening, I sometimes meet my friends. We often eat pizza or salad. I don’t work at the weekend. On Saturdays, I clean my flat and go shopping. On Sundays, I relax at home.
6. Where is Sarah from?
7. What is Sarah’s job?
8. How does she go to work?
9. What can Sarah do at work?
10. What does she do on Saturdays?
